TITUSJANE HIGH SCHOOL GALLERY — 50 IMAGE SLOTS

Files:
- gallery.html
- gallery.css
- gallery.js

Copy these three files into your Titusjane website project folder, replacing the old gallery files.

Image folder:
images/gallery/

Add your 50 photos using these exact filenames:
school1.jpg through school50.jpg

Example:
images/gallery/school1.jpg
images/gallery/school2.jpg
...
images/gallery/school50.jpg

If your school logo is at images/school-logo.png, it will appear in the gallery header.
Open gallery.html through your local server, for example:
http://localhost:3000/gallery.html

The page includes 50 image slots but does not include 50 real photos. You need to put your own photos in the image folder.
